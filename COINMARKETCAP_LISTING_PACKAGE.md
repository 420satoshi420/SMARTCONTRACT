> **Full Detailed Description (200-300 words):**

>  
> The New World ($NEWWORLD) token solves traditional payment and booking inefficiencies in tourism by providing:  
> 1. **Zero-FX Direct Checkout:** Foreign tourists can seamlessly book and pay for wellness packages using $NEWWORLD with gasless EIP-2612 approvals, avoiding expensive credit card exchange fees.
